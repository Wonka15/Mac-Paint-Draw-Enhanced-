Edit Points

Choose a vector shape, then drag its points or handles right on the canvas.

  • Bezier Curve: click-drag each anchor to pull its handles.
  • Finish: press Enter or double-click.
  • Edit Points: drag an anchor or handle to refine the curve.
  • Option-drag handle: move it independently.
  • Click selected path: add an anchor.
Tip: hold Option while dragging a handle to move it independently.

Shape Builder

Beginner steps
1. Draw two overlapping Vector rect or Vector oval shapes.
2. Choose Shape Builder. Click the first shape, then hold Shift and click the second. The blue outline shows what is selected.
3. Pick an action below. Start with Build / Unite to join the shapes.
Tip: this version combines whole shapes, not individual overlap pieces.
